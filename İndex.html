A little expert advice

What makes a great business name?

A thoughtful name is more than a label. It sets expectations, gives people something to remember, and provides a foundation for your visual identity.

Keep it simple and memorable

Look for a name that’s easy to say, spell, and share. Short, distinctive words are often easier for customers to recall.

Make room to grow

Choose a name that reflects your personality without boxing your business into one product or a passing trend.

Do your homework

Before committing, search for existing businesses, trademarks, social handles, and domain availability in your relevant markets.

Good to know

Frequently asked questions

A few helpful answers for the journey from first idea to finished brand.

Is the brand name generator free to use?

Yes. You can generate ideas, copy names, and save favorites for free, with no account required.

How are the name ideas generated?

The tool combines your keyword or chosen niche with words and patterns associated with your selected style. Each click shuffles the combinations to give you a new set of ideas.

Can I use these names for my business?

The ideas are a starting point, not a guarantee of availability or ownership. Check business registrations, trademarks, domains, and social handles before choosing a name. Consider professional legal advice when appropriate.

Where are my saved names stored?

Favorites are stored locally in your browser on this device. They won’t automatically sync across devices and may be removed if you clear browser data.

Are the logo and color suggestions final designs?

They’re simple visual prompts to help you explore a direction. Use them as inspiration for a designer or as a starting point for developing your own brand identity.
